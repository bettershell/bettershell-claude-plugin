# The honest review

`review_resume` measures the résumé the way BetterShell lays out a page and returns facts: how much of a page it fills, which jobs have one line, lines with no number or result, lines that open with what the person was assigned rather than what they did, lines that run long, missing contact details, and, with a posting, tools and terms the posting names that the résumé never does. It saves nothing.

## How to deliver it

- **Blunt and specific, never cruel.** Quote the line, say what's wrong with it, say what would make it strong.
- **Facts first, then judgment.** Lead with what BetterShell measured; add your own reading of what a recruiter sees in ten seconds.
- **Say what's strong too.** A review that finds nothing good isn't believed.
- **No score out of 100, no grade, no percentage.** They mean nothing and people know it.
- **Separate what can be fixed now from what only they know.** Wording, order and length can be fixed now (`tailor_resume` makes a finished page for a job). Results, numbers and missing work need their answers: ask one thing at a time.

## End with an offer

"I can fix the wording and make a page for the job you're after now. For the rest I need a few answers from you. Start?"
