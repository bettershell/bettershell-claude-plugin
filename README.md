# BetterShell for Claude

BetterShell, the résumé builder, as a Claude plugin: the BetterShell connector plus four skills that tell Claude how to use it well.

- **tailor-a-resume**: a finished, tailored one-page PDF from your résumé and a job.
- **review-a-resume**: an honest, specific review from BetterShell's measurements of the page. No scores.
- **build-the-profile**: an interview, one job at a time, saved to your BetterShell profile in your words.
- **job-search**: your applications kept as you talk, follow-ups, interview prep from the résumé you sent, and postings that fit your profile.

The connector is `https://bettershell.ai/mcp`. Making and reviewing résumés needs no account; the profile and the job search use your BetterShell account, connected when you ask.

More: https://bettershell.ai/bettershell-in-chatgpt-and-claude · Privacy: https://bettershell.ai/privacy

This folder is published as its own public repository so Claude's plugin directory can read it. Its source of truth is `chat_plugin/` in the BetterShell repository (`CHAT_CONNECTOR.md` §7).
